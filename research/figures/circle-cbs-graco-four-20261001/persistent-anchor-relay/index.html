The old persistent response path disabled the original CBS anchor relay. Incoming anchor factors are excluded from the GLOBAL covariance graph, so anchors learned only through those factors could not produce a local marginal and were silently omitted. Sparse chains exposed the bug; the earlier fully connected three-robot tests did not.

The repair forwards cached anchor Gaussians with their original producer, mean, covariance and frame. It does not label forwarded evidence as an independent local measurement or damp it a second time. Repeated unchanged relays and echoes to the original producer are suppressed. Local-belief damping, the two covariance exclusions, random stage selection and final validation gates remain unchanged.

The new native five-robot chain test fails on the old core and passes after the repair. Tests also cover independent stages, reordered delivery, a ring, producer/covariance preservation, duplicate suppression and graph-size stability. Real DDS tests cover a growing five-robot chain with both pose-only graphs and GPU registration factors.
